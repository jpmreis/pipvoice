Pip

Your family's voice messages, one tap away. Put Pip on your home screen: it's also the only way its little notifications can reach you.

  1. Tap the … button in the toolbar
  2. Tap Share
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add, then open Pip from your home screen
  5. Make sure to enable notifications to receive messages from your family
